DOOM (Rust + Bevy engine)
=========================

1. Start doom_launcher.exe.

2. Pick your game. The launcher looks for IWADs next to it, in the WADS
   folder, in DOOMWADDIR, and in Steam and GOG installs of DOOM. If yours
   isn't listed, click Browse... and select the .wad file from your own copy
   (doom.wad, doom2.wad, tnt.wad, plutonia.wad ...), or drop it on the window.
   No copy? Freedoom (https://freedoom.github.io/) is free.

3. Optional: add mods (.wad, .pk3, .deh, .bex) with Add... or by dropping
   them on the window. They load top to bottom; later ones override earlier
   ones.

4. Optional: a start map (E1M1, MAP01 ...) and skill, fullscreen, the
   classic renderer, monster options. Leave the map empty for the title
   screen and menu.

5. Click LAUNCH.

The launcher remembers your choices in launcher.cfg. The game's messages go
to doom_log.txt; if the game stops with an error, the launcher shows them.

Keep doom_launcher.exe, doom_bevy.exe and the RESOURCES folder together.
doom_bevy.exe can also be started directly; run "doom_bevy.exe --help" in a
terminal for every option.

Controls: WASD or arrows move, mouse looks, Ctrl/left mouse fires,
Space/E uses, 1-7 pick weapons, Tab automap, Esc menu, ` console,
Alt+Enter fullscreen, F12 screenshot.
